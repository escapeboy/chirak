Own tickets

533 of the shop's own tickets, masked and frozen as a separate slice. They came with labels made by people: the dataset authors Georgieva-Trifonova, Stefanova and Kalchev (Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/TXIK9P, CC BY 4.0): the 182 test reviews of the MTEB release plus the 351 reviews of the check half of its train split. Neither the judge nor Chirak changed them. The tickets themselves are private to the run owner.

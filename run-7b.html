Own tickets

182 of the shop's own tickets, masked and frozen as a separate slice. They came with labels made by people: the dataset authors Georgieva-Trifonova, Stefanova and Kalchev (Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/TXIK9P, CC BY 4.0), test split of the MTEB release. Neither the judge nor Chirak changed them. The tickets themselves are private to the run owner.
